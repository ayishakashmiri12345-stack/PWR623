# Submit an Assignment on Blackboard

A beginner's guide for first-year Towson University students.

## Welcome

Starting college involves learning new digital tools. This guide helps first-year Towson students who are unfamiliar with Blackboard Ultra submit coursework with greater confidence.

By following this guide, you will learn how to:

1. Locate an assignment in your course.
2. Read the assignment instructions and requirements.
3. Attach the correct file.
4. Submit your assignment.
5. Confirm that Blackboard received your work.

## The Communication Problem
First year Towson Universty students are adjusting to a new enviroment and lot of new techonlogies. It can be overwhelming for them to understand a platform such as Blackboard which will be where they are going to submitting all their assignments.

## Audeince 
This guide is for Towson Universty undergraduate students who are entering their very first year of college and have never used Blackboard before. 

## Before You Begin

You will need:
- Your Towson NetID and password.
- Access to your Blackboard course.
- A completed assignment saved on your computer.
- A reliable internet connection.
