# Understanding Towson Universty's Blackboard Ulta

Blackboard Ultra is the learning management system used at Towson University. Students can access course materials, professor instructions, review assignments,take exams,submit coursework, view professor feedback, and check grades.

## Important Terms to search for on Blackboard

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Course Content | The area containing your course materials and assignments. |
| Assignment | The task your instructor has asked you to complete. |
| Due Date | The deadline for completing an assignment. |
| Submission | The work you send to your instructor through Blackboard. |
| Submission Receipt | Confirmation that Blackboard received your work. |

## Why The Blackboard System is Essential

Blackboard is the main way that assingments are submitted and graded for feedback. Blackboard is also how students view assignments,instrustions, grades, class syllabus, and announcments from both professors and peers.


## What You Need

Before submitting an assignment:

1. Sign in to Blackboard with your Towson credentials.
2. Find your course on Blackboard
2. Make sure your course is available.
4. Find youe assignment with the instructions.
3. Complete your assignment.
4. Save your work in the file format required by your instructor.
