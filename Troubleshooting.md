## Resolving Issues that can Occur
## My File Is Missing

Before submitting, check the attached filename. If the wrong file appears, remove it and attach the correct document. 

## My File Type Is Not Accepted

Read your instructor's requirements and check whether your assignment must be submitted as a PDF, Word document, or another file type. Save a copy in the required format and try reuploading it. 

## My File Will Not Upload

Check your internet connection to ensure what you submit can properly upload. Also confirm that your file is not too large.If this problem continues, contact Towson Blackboard Support.
 
## I Am Not Sure My Assignment Was Submitted

Return to the assignment and check its submission status or available receipt. Reupload if not submitted and make sure when submitting you do not close the webpage to early. If Blackboard does not show that your work was received, contact your instructor or Towson Blackboard Support.
